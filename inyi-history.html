🌿 THE HISTORY OF INYI

From the abandoned child to Umu-egeshi Agbariji — My Root

Part 1: Who Was Inyi?

Before Nkwo Inyi, there was a child born in Agunese, near Umueji in Ufuma. His upper teeth came before lower — considered aru. Abandoned at Aguezi forest near Mmam River. A man from Nkwere Ubaha found him and raised him...

Full story you wrote: Endurance with soldier ants, banishment, living under INYI TREE, farmer Ji-Ala naming him INYI, marrying Ukagbantu, becoming INYI OMIRE with 5 sons: Ome, Enugwu, Obune, Amankwo, Agbariji.

Part 2: The Four Villages After

Arum - daughter of Agbariji, Umuagu - adopted by Amankwo, Nkwere - migrants from Nkwere Ubaha, Akwu - migrants from Akwu Achi hosted by Agbariji.

🌟 FIRST RECORD: UMU-EGESHI - Agbariji Inyi

Location: Between Nkwo Inyi-Agbariji and Akpuugo-eze. Neighbour to Umu-Chukwu.

Landmark: Famous Achii and Isi-nwa Achii stream.

Family: Udeafuta family, Elder Dan Udeh. First to establish borehole in Umu-egeshi.

Churches: Jesus Flag Mission etc.

This page is now the first online record of Umu-egeshi. If you are from here, add your history.
