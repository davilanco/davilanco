Who Is Davilanco?

I am Davilanco, an Afrobeats artist from Umu-egeshi, Agbariji Inyi in Oji River LGA, Enugu State.

Born into the Udeafuta family. My music carries the endurance, courage and strength of Inyi Omire — the same spirit of the child who survived the forest at Aguezi.

My sound is street anthem meets origin story. From the Achii stream to the studio.
